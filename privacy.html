privacy

What this site collects, and what I do with it.

Last updated September 29, 2026

The short version: if you join the email list, I get your email address so I can send you the Family AI Safety guide when it's ready. I don't sell it, share it, or use it for ads. You can unsubscribe anytime, and you can ask me to delete it.

Who I am

AI Actually is a small site run by me, Anthony Proctor, in Denver, Colorado. You can reach me at hello@heyaiactually.com.

What I collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up. The signup form sends it to Kit, the email service I use. Kit also keeps the usual record of the signup, like when it happened.
  • Visit counts, with no cookies. I use GoatCounter to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It records the page, the site that sent you, your browser type, screen size, and a rough location based on your internet address. GoatCounter says it doesn't store internet addresses and doesn't put cookies or anything else in your browser.
  • Emails you send me. If you write to me, I get your message and your address, and I use them to reply.

That's it. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, and no accounts on this site.

Why I collect it

Your email address is for sending you the Family AI Safety guide, plus any later emails from AI Actually. Every one has an unsubscribe link. Visit counts tell me which parts of the site are useful. I don't use either for anything else.

Who handles it

  • Kit (kit.com) stores the email list and sends the emails. Its own privacy policy covers how it handles your data.
  • GoatCounter (goatcounter.com) counts visits. Its privacy policy explains what it keeps.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub says it logs visitors' internet addresses for security, as most web hosts do. See the GitHub privacy statement.

I don't sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone else.

Confirming, unsubscribing, and deleting

  • You confirm first. After you sign up, Kit sends one email asking you to confirm. Nothing else arrives unless you click it.
  • Unsubscribe anytime. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.
  • Ask me to delete it. Email hello@heyaiactually.com and I'll delete your address from Kit. You can also ask me what I have on you.

Children

This site is for adults. It isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect email addresses from them. If you think a child signed up, email me and I'll delete the address.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
