the problem

This isn't a future problem. The FBI is already counting it.

$893 million

Losses in complaints to the FBI in 2025 that mentioned AI, across more than 22,000 complaints.

$7.7 billion

Total reported losses to online crime for people 60 and older in 2025, across 201,266 complaints. That's every kind of crime, not only AI.

Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, 2025 Internet Crime Report (PDF). These are only the cases people reported. AARP's summary of the report is a shorter read.

what we're building

Three kinds of protection in one AI that works for you.

1 · calls and fakes

Scam calls, cloned voices, and deepfakes

You can't tell what's real anymore.

Built to help you check a call, text, or message that doesn't add up. Forward it or describe it, and get a plain answer and what to do next, before you pay a thing.

2 · your own AI

AI tools that act without asking

The AI just does what you tell it.

Built to watch the AI apps and agents you already use, and to stop and check with you before one of them buys, sends, or shares something you didn't approve.

3 · chatbots and companions

Chatbots that get too close

It's just being friendly.

Built to notice when an AI chat is pushing you, flattering you into sharing more than you meant to, or steering you somewhere you didn't choose.

why we're building it

If AI is going to be used against us, we need one on our side.

I'm Anthony Proctor. I build with AI every day, so I know how good it is. I also know how easily it can be turned on you and the people around you. A voice can be copied. An AI tool can spend money before you notice. A chatbot can get close enough that you tell it things you'd never tell a stranger.

Most of the advice out there asks you to be on guard all the time. That's not fair to anyone. So we're building one AI whose whole job is to look out for you. I'd rather ship something small that works than promise something big that doesn't.

// Anthony Proctor, founder. Denver, Colorado.

questions

What people ask us.

What is AI Actually?

A personal AI we're building to protect you from AI used against you: scam calls and cloned voices, AI tools that act without asking, and chatbots that push too far. One helper on your side, instead of a separate fix for each problem.

Is it available?

Not yet. It's in development, and there's nothing to download today. People on the early access list get first access when there's a version to try.

What will it cost?

We haven't decided. Early access members will hear about pricing first, before anything is for sale.

Will I need to be technical?

No. We're designing it for people who'd rather not think about AI at all.

What happens to my email address?

We use Kit to send email. It asks you to confirm your address first, every message has a one click unsubscribe, and we won't sell or share your address. The privacy policy has the details.

Who's building it?

Anthony Proctor, a builder in Denver, Colorado. AI Actually is independent. It takes no money or referral fees from any AI company or security product.
